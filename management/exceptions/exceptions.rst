Exceptions
============

Tailoring exceptions — cases where a project executes the org-level
workflow differently than :doc:`the process meta-model
<../../organisation/governance/framework/process_metamodel>` describes — tracked
as ``exception`` needs, one entry per deviation, per the ``tailors``
relationship between the project-level and org-level workflow shown in
that diagram.
