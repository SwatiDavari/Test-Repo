Strategy
========

Purpose
-------

Define the enterprise software engineering vision,
business goals and strategic principles.

Vision
------

Create a reusable, traceable and compliant
software delivery ecosystem that enables all
products to be developed, verified and released
consistently.

Business Objectives
-------------------

OBJ_001
   Reduce engineering duplication across products.

OBJ_002
   Ensure full traceability from requirement
   to release evidence.

OBJ_003
   Accelerate product onboarding through
   reusable engineering capabilities.

OBJ_004
   Achieve compliance readiness by default.

Engineering Principles
----------------------

PRIN_001
   Reuse before create.

PRIN_002
   Verification drives release readiness.

PRIN_003
   Automation over manual processes.

PRIN_004
   Every artifact is traceable.

PRIN_005
   Compliance is built-in.

Success Metrics
---------------

METRIC_001
   Product onboarding < 2 weeks

METRIC_002
   100% requirement traceability

METRIC_003
   100% automated release evidence

METRIC_004
   < 5% engineering asset duplication
