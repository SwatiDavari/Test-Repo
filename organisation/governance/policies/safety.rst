Safety Policy
================

Statement
---------

This organization treats functional safety as a non-negotiable
organizational value, independent of any single product's schedule or
budget pressure. No product-level safety work (HARA, safety goals,
FSR/TSR/SSR) is considered valid unless the organizational prerequisites
below are in place first, per ISO 26262 Part 2.

The detailed, individually-tracked requirements that implement this policy
live in :doc:`../functionalsafety/org_fusa_requirements` (safety culture, safety
management system, competence, QMS alignment, confirmation measures,
field monitoring).

Scope
-----

Applies to every project developing or maintaining a safety-related
E/E system under this organization, regardless of ASIL.

Ownership
---------

Functional safety organizational requirements are owned at the
organization level, not the project level — a project may tailor *how*
it satisfies them (see ``ORG_SMS_002``) but may not skip them.
