ISO/IEC/IEEE 15288 — Organizational Project-Enabling Processes
====================================================================

The six Organizational Project-Enabling Processes (clause 6.2) — the
organization-level processes that exist so a project doesn't have to
invent its own life cycle model, infrastructure, staffing, or knowledge
capture from scratch. Two are already satisfied by pages that predate
this one; four had no home anywhere in the repo until now.
